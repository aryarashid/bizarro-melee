Melee Advanced Tech Reference

  • SHFFL: Short-Hop (O or tap Jump) + Aerial + Fast-Fall (Down at apex) + L-Cancel (L/Z within 7f of landing).
  • Wavedash / Waveland: Jump + diagonal down + Hard Shield (L) right after takeoff to slide across the stage.
  • Light Shield vs Power-Shield: Hold H or light-press L/R trigger for big slip-away Light Shield; tap Hard Shield within 4f to Power-Shield & reflect!
  • Shield Drop: On a platform while shielding, tilt stick down slightly to drop straight through into an aerial!
  • JC Grab & JC Up-Smash: Press Jump out of dash, then Grab (U) or Up+Attack during jump-squat for sliding punishes.
  • SDI / ASDI Down: Flick stick during hitlag to escape multi-hits, or hold Down on final hitlag frame to stick to the ground!
  • Edge Cancel & B-Reverse: Slide off platform edges during lag to cancel immediately; flick opposite direction on frames 1-3 of Neutral/Down-B to flip momentum!
